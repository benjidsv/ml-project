# Battery State-of-Health Prediction from Charge Curves

**Authors:** Benjamin Arbousset, Loïc Cupif, Aurélien Ridard — DE INGE-2 2027, EFREI Paris  
**Course:** Machine Learning for Data Engineers (Instructor: Stephany Rajeh, Ph.D.)

A two-phase study on predicting lithium-ion battery State-of-Health (SOH) from the shape of individual charge cycles, without any discharge measurement at inference time.

> **Full writeup:** [study.pdf](study.pdf) — covers methodology, all experiments, failed approaches, and analysis.

---

## Results

| Phase | Best model | MAE | Setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — NASA only (13 cells, ~800 samples) | Random Forest | 0.032 | LOBO-13 |
| Phase 2 — NASA + CALCE (22 cells, 12 260 samples) | BiGRU + rate-warp | 0.038 | GroupKFold-6 |
| Phase 2 — cross-dataset transfer | BiGRU + rate-warp | 0.056 | LODO mean (10-seed) |

DL models match RF once data reaches ~12k samples. BiGRU with rate-warp augmentation is the only architecture that transfers reliably across datasets (hold-CALCE MAE 0.050, hold-NASA MAE 0.061).

---

## Datasets

- **NASA PCoE** — 13 retained cells (B0005–B0018 controlled, RW series randomized). Raw `.mat` files in `data/nasa/` (not committed — download from [NASA PCoE](https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/)).
- **CALCE CS2/CX2** — 10 retained cells, Arbin Excel exports. Files in `data/calce/` (not committed).
- **Processed tensor** — `data/processed/vg_extended_tier1.npz` (22 cells, 12 260 × 128 × 3, voltage-grid representation). Generated by `notebooks/extended-dataset/01_build_extended_dataset.ipynb`.
